Qadam is a task and habit tracker. Plan your day, mark what you did, and watch your streak grow week after week.

Everything you need to keep going, nothing that slows you down

  • Plan the day

    Tasks grouped into overdue, today and done, so you always know what is next.

  • Protect your streak

    See how many days in a row you kept a habit, and don't break the chain.

  • Focus in sessions

    Work in focused blocks and see how many hours you really spent.

How it works

  1. Add a habit

    Pick a name, a time of day and how often you want to do it.

  2. Check in daily

    Each check-in fills a day on your calendar.

  3. Watch the progress

    Streaks and focus time show how far you have come.
